Effective cleaning comes down to **fixed timing, correct method, and adequate contact time**. This schedule slots cleaning and disinfection into specific time blocks that line up with the *Kennel Work Schedule*. The core principle never changes: **clean first (remove organic matter), then disinfect (kill pathogens) — and it only counts once the contact time is met.**

## Daily Cleaning & Disinfection Schedule

| Time | Task | Method | Disinfectant / contact time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 07:00 – 07:45 | Morning dry clean-up | Scoop waste, remove leftover food and soiled bedding, sweep hair (dry before wet) | — |
| 07:45 – 08:30 | Bowls (AM) | Wash, soak in disinfectant, rinse and dry | QUAT, 10 min |
| 09:45 – 11:00 | Main kennel clean | Move dog out → scrub floor, wall base, gate with detergent → rinse | Routine QUAT 10 min, rinse and dry |
| 13:00 – 14:00 | Kennel disinfection (key) | Apply disinfectant to already-cleaned runs, room/zone by zone | Accelerated H₂O₂ 5–10 min |
| 15:15 – 16:00 | Bowls (PM) + yard clean | Wash/disinfect bowls; clear yard waste | QUAT 10 min |
| 17:00 – 17:45 | Drains / walkways | Flush drains, clear walkways, check for pooling water | Disinfect as needed, always dry |

> Contact time is non-negotiable: rinsing off immediately = no disinfection. Use a timer. Bleach and some disinfectants must be rinsed off after to avoid burning paws.

## Weekly Deep Clean Schedule

| Day | Deep-clean task | Disinfectant / contact time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mon** | K1, K2, K4 full deep disinfection + full bedding change; K3 first 50 kennels | Potassium peroxymonosulfate 10 min |
| **Tue** | K3 remaining 50 kennels deep-cleaned (kept apart from the vet round) | Potassium peroxymonosulfate 10 min |
| **Wed** | Deep clean the yards; disinfect fencing and high-touch gate handles | Accelerated H₂O₂ 5–10 min |
| **Thu** | Deep flush drainage system; dust walls, ceilings and vents | As needed |
| **Fri** | Disinfect storeroom and cleaning tools; review disinfectant stock and dilutions | QUAT 10 min |
| **Sat / Sun** | Maintain the daily routine; handle issues as they arise, no extra deep tasks | — |

## Disinfectant Quick Reference

| Disinfectant | Kills | Contact time | Key note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| QUAT (quaternary ammonium) | General bacteria, some viruses (**NOT parvovirus**) | ~10 min | Inactivated by organic matter/soap; routine daily use |
| Bleach (sodium hypochlorite) | Almost all pathogens including parvo | ~10 min | Parvo dilution **1:32** (~30 ml per litre); mix fresh, rinse and ventilate after |
| Accelerated hydrogen peroxide | Broad spectrum incl. parvo, kennel cough | ~5–10 min | Gentle, easy to rinse — **first choice for the puppy area** |
| Potassium peroxymonosulfate | Broad spectrum incl. parvo, tolerant of organic load | ~10 min | Common for deep cleaning / outbreaks |

## Isolation / Outbreak (additional)

| Trigger | Action | Disinfectant / contact time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vomiting, diarrhoea or coughing | Isolate immediately, dedicated tools, cleaned last by one person | Bleach 1:32 or peroxymonosulfate, 10 min |
| Kennel with parvo history | Thorough clean then enhanced disinfection; re-check for months | Bleach 1:32, 10 min, rinse and dry |
| Every contact with a different dog group | Wash/sanitise hands, change or disinfect footwear | Hand sanitiser |

## Extra Precautions

1. **Never mix chemicals**: bleach + ammonia cleaner = toxic gas; bleach + QUAT cancel each other out. Use one at a time, rinse thoroughly before switching.
2. **Clean before you disinfect**: organic residue disables QUATs and bleach — the order can't be reversed.
3. **Always rinse + dry**: prevents paw burns and stops moisture breeding bacteria/mould.
4. **Don't pressure-hose contaminated waste**: it aerosolises pathogens — dry clean-up first.
5. **Zone-dedicated, colour-coded tools**: each site, the puppy area and isolation each use their own tools, never shared.
6. **Puppy area (K4) is strictest**: accelerated hydrogen peroxide, thorough rinsing, dedicated tools, ventilate before returning puppies.
7. **Chemical safety**: store separately, keep original labels, post dilutions on the wall, wear PPE (gloves, plus goggles/mask as needed).
