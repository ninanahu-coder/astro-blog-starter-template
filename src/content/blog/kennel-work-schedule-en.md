FluffyWA currently operates **four kennel sites (K1–K4)** across Canning Vale and Southern River, caring for approximately **160 adult dogs and 15 puppies** in total. To keep every site running smoothly with limited staffing, the following work schedule applies. All staff must follow the timetable for their assigned site and keep daily records.

## Staffing Levels

Each site is rostered to the following staffing standard:

| Time window | Staffing | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 09:00 – 12:00 | **1.5 staff** | 1 full shift + 1 half shift; all heavy work happens here |
| 13:00 – 16:00 | **1.5 staff** | 1 full shift + 1 half shift; all heavy work happens here |
| Other hours (07:00–09:00, 12:00–13:00, 16:00–18:00) | **0.5 staff** | One duty person on half time; light tasks only (patrol, water top-up) |

**Rostering principle**: all labour-intensive or two-person work (kennel cleaning, feeding, exercise, disinfection, grooming) must be completed within the two 1.5-staff windows. Heavy work is strictly prohibited during 0.5-staff windows — the duty person patrols and observes, records and reports issues, and never handles them alone.

**Downgrade order when short-staffed** (when there are last-minute absences and floater cover can't fill the gap, protect the basics in this priority order):

1. **Protect feeding and water** — the two daily meals (puppies on the puppy plan) and all-day drinking water cannot be skipped
2. **Protect health patrols** — at least one room-by-room patrol per high-staff window; acute cases must never be missed
3. **Protect cleaning and disinfection** — at minimum clear kennels soiled by waste; deep disinfection may be deferred but must be noted in the log
4. **May be temporarily compressed** — shorten the second exercise round or switch it to in-kennel settling; reschedule grooming appointments (notify the customer in advance)

> **K3 works zone by zone, no skipping**: K3's 100 kennels are split into four zones — A, B, C, D — of 25 each. Cleaning, feeding, exercise and disinfection always roll A→B→C→D, with the lead marking each zone's completion time in the log. Even when short-handed, finish zone by zone to avoid the "half done here, half done there" gaps.

## Daily Timetable (all sites)

### 07:00 – 09:00 | Morning duty (0.5 staff)

| Time | Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| 07:00 – 07:45 | Opening patrol: check every dog's condition, log anomalies, phone in emergencies immediately |
| 07:45 – 08:30 | Top up all water, switch on ventilation fans (check heaters in winter) |
| 08:30 – 09:00 | Verify today's check-in/check-out list and feeding charts, prep materials for the morning |

> **What to look for on the first opening patrol**: focus on the signals left overnight — each kennel's stool condition (any diarrhoea or blood), any vomiting, leftover food from last night, whether the water has been knocked over, and whether the dog's alertness and standing posture look normal. At the first sign of anything off, log it and isolate if needed, then move on to the next kennel; never skip the patrol just to rush into feeding.

## Weekly Workflow

The daily routine (patrol, feeding, cleaning, exercise, disinfection) stays fixed; the table below lists each day's **focus tasks**, all scheduled within the 1.5-staff windows:

| Day | Morning core block (9:00–12:00) | Afternoon core block (13:00–16:00) | Reception (K4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mon** | Deep clean: K1, K2, K4 full disinfection and bedding change; K3 first 50 kennels | Continue deep cleaning; compile last week's logs | 9–11, 16–18 |
| **Tue** | K3 remaining 50 kennels deep-cleaned | Verify vaccination and worming records; vet round (route K4 → K3 → K2 → K1) | 9–11, 16–18 |
| **Wed** | Stocktake at every site: food, medication, cleaning supplies | Consolidate at K4 and submit the purchase order; receive and shelve last week's order | 9–11, 16–18 |
| **Thu** | Equipment checks: fencing, crates, water systems (K3 first) | Equipment checks: ventilation fans, heaters; follow up repairs | 9–11, 16–18 |
| **Fri** | Process Adoption Portal applications | Update boarding files, customer follow-up calls; confirm weekend booking list | 9–11, 16–18 |
| **Sat** | Reception peak: booked check-ins/check-outs | Extra exercise round (higher weekend occupancy) | 10–11, 16–18 |
| **Sun** | Normal care only, no extra tasks | Review next week's roster; prep Monday's deep-clean materials | 17–18 only |

**Weekly flow notes:**

- Deep disinfection (Mon–Tue) and the vet round (Tue) are kept in different time blocks so disinfectant odours don't interfere with examinations
- Wednesday's purchase order arrives before the following Wednesday — a one-week cycle
- Friday must confirm the weekend booking list and lock in weekend staffing
- Sunday is the lightest day: wrap up and prepare for the coming week
- Set a **safety stock line** for inventory: when food, common medications or cleaning/disinfectant supplies fall below one week's usage, flag them in red on Wednesday's order for priority restocking — don't wait until you run out

### Handover Quick-Check List

At the end of every shift (especially the evening lock-up and after cross-site support), confirm each item below and hand over face-to-face or by phone — never from memory:

- [ ] Today's per-dog records are complete (food intake, water, waste, alertness, medication, exercise, anomalies)
- [ ] Dogs needing ongoing observation/isolation are flagged in the log and named verbally to the next shift
- [ ] Unfinished or deferred tasks (deep disinfection, repairs, rescheduled grooming) have their reason and follow-up written down
- [ ] Bowls washed, water topped up, disinfection tools returned to their assigned storage zones
- [ ] Door locks, fencing, yard gates and ventilation/climate-control equipment checked
- [ ] Next day's check-in/check-out and drop-off/pick-up booking list verified

## Guidelines

1. **Health first**: isolate and report any dog showing poor appetite, diarrhoea or coughing to the site lead immediately; call the vet if needed. During 0.5-staff windows, phone in first and follow instructions — never handle it alone.
2. **Puppy care**: the 15 puppies stay at K4 with small frequent meals and a separate play area; puppies not fully vaccinated must not contact visiting dogs.
3. **Feeding rules**: strictly follow each dog's feeding chart, honouring owners' special dietary requirements (puppies, seniors, allergies).
4. **Exercise guarantee**: every dog gets two exercise rounds daily, 15–30 minutes each, always within 1.5-staff windows; large and small dogs exercise separately; close every gate behind you.
5. **Temperature management**: keep ventilation fans running in summer; run and check heaters daily in winter.
6. **Disinfection regime**: bowls washed daily, kennels disinfected daily, yards deep-cleaned weekly; staff moving between sites must change or disinfect footwear.
7. **Complete records**: daily logs per site must be accurate; handovers at K4 reception are signed face-to-face within reception hours; inter-site transfers require sign-off from both site leads.
8. **Emergency plan**: emergency contacts (0405 098 765 / admin@fluffywa.com.au) are posted in every duty room; report any incident to the site lead on duty immediately.

For roster changes or inter-site support, apply to the manager at least two days ahead so that every K1–K4 time slot stays covered. Thank you for your hard work — together we make FluffyWA a safe, clean and warm home for the dogs!
