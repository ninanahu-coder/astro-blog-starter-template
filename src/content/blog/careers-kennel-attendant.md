FluffyWA is a growing pet care company operating **four kennel sites** across Canning Vale and Southern River, caring for around 160 dogs and 15 puppies. We're looking for reliable, animal-loving people to join our team as **Kennel Attendants**.

## Why work with us

- **Fixed, predictable shifts** — your roster is published a week ahead, no on-call surprises
- **Clear progression path**: Floater → Site Keeper → Site Lead
- **Paid under the Animal Care and Veterinary Services Award**, casual rates include 25% loading
- Work with a small, supportive team that puts animal welfare first

## The roles

| Role | Hours | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Site Keeper | 09:00 – 16:00 (1 h lunch) | Part-time / full-time |
| Morning Floater | 07:00 – 13:00 | Casual / part-time |
| Evening Floater | 13:00 – 18:00 | Casual / part-time |

Weekend availability is essential for keeper roles — weekends are our reception peak.

## What you'll do

- Feed dogs to individual feeding charts, including puppy meal plans
- Clean and disinfect kennels, wash bowls, change bedding
- Run supervised exercise sessions in our yards (15–30 minute batches)
- Monitor health and behaviour, log and report anything unusual
- Help with reception at our Ranford Rd site: check-ins, pick-ups, customer questions
- Keep accurate daily records and hand over properly at shift end

## What we're looking for

- Genuine comfort around dogs of all sizes and temperaments — including the messy parts of the job
- Physically fit: this is an active, outdoor role in all weather
- Reliable early starts (morning shifts open at 7:00 am)
- Experience with animals (paid, volunteer, or farm) highly regarded
- A Certificate II/III in Animal Care Services is a plus, not a must — we train on the job
- Driver's licence preferred (our four sites are a short drive apart)

**Students welcome**: we support TAFE Animal Care placement hours, and strong performers are offered ongoing roles.

## How to apply

Email **admin@fluffywa.com.au** with a short note about your experience with animals and which shifts suit you, or call **0405 098 765**. Shortlisted applicants are invited to a paid trial shift (9:00 am – 12:00 pm) at one of our sites — we believe half a day working alongside the team beats any interview.
