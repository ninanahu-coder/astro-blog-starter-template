A safe, stable baseline plan for the daily operation of four kennels (K1–K4, roughly 160 adult dogs + 15 puppies), turning "welfare + disease prevention + compliance" into concrete actions that can be carried out every day. Every item carries a source tag: **[RSPCA]** RSPCA recommendation · **[NSW Code No 5]** NSW Code of Practice No 5 · **[VIC Code]** Victorian boarding code · **[Shelter-medicine best practice]** industry best practice (not law) · **[Verify with local council]** governed by your council's kennel licence.

> **Two compliance premises that must be stated up front:**
>
> ① **WA has no dedicated mandatory boarding-kennel code** — WA operates under the *Animal Welfare Act 2002* + each **local council's "Kennel Establishment Licence" conditions**, which are the rules that legally bind the four sites. **Each site must separately verify its own council's licence conditions**, and those take precedence over this plan. RSPCA itself does not issue a WA-specific standard; instead it points to the Five Domains and the state codes.

> ② The specific figures in this plan are compiled from searches of NSW COP No 5, the Victorian boarding code, and the RSPCA knowledge base; **verify against the official text before writing them into SOPs**. Items tagged [Shelter-medicine best practice] are common industry practice, **not a mandatory requirement of RSPCA or Australian law**.

## 1. Cleaning and Disinfection

### Frequency (the non-negotiable rhythm)

- **Clean kennels and activity areas at least once daily** [NSW Code No 5] [VIC Code] — hose down, remove faeces, change bedding, wash food and water bowls
- **Disinfect at least once weekly**; in addition, **disinfection is mandatory before a new dog moves in and after a disease outbreak** [NSW Code No 5]
  - ⚠️ This is a **regulatory floor, not our operating standard**. FluffyWA disinfects kennels **daily** per the *Cleaning and Disinfection Schedule* (the 13:00–14:00 slot), well above this floor. **On the floor, always follow the Cleaning and Disinfection Schedule**; this line only states the compliance minimum.
- **Perform a terminal deep clean and disinfection at turnover** [RSPCA] [Shelter-medicine best practice]
- Remove faeces and urine **as soon as they appear**, not on a fixed schedule [Shelter-medicine best practice]

### Correct sequence: clean first, then disinfect

- **First use hot water + detergent to thoroughly wash out organic matter, then disinfect** — organic matter deactivates disinfectant [Shelter-medicine best practice]
- Allow enough **contact time** (per the product label), then rinse and dry
- Choose disinfectant "for suitability, safety, and effectiveness" [NSW Code No 5]

### Disinfectants and pathogens (critical)

- **Quaternary ammonium compounds (quats) are unreliable against canine parvovirus**; for parvo use **accelerated hydrogen peroxide**, or **bleach at about 1:30 with a contact time of about 10 min** (effective only on non-porous surfaces already cleaned of organic matter) [Shelter-medicine best practice]
- If any site also houses cats: **avoid pine tar / phenol / cresol / chloroxylenol-type** disinfectants, which are toxic to cats [NSW Code No 5]

### Preventing cross-contamination

- **Cleaning order: healthy puppies → healthy adults → sick dogs → isolation area last**; within a zone, do the most vulnerable first [Shelter-medicine best practice]
- **Zone your equipment and colour-code it** (dedicated mop / bucket / disinfectant per zone); healthy and isolation zones never share [Shelter-medicine best practice]
- After cleaning the isolation / sick zone, change protective clothing and gloves, and wash hands
- Internal surfaces must be **impervious, durable, and washable**, with floor–wall joints sealed and floors that drain well [NSW Code No 5]; **keep dogs dry** while cleaning

## 2. Feeding and Water

- **Feed adult dogs at least once daily** [NSW Code No 5] [VIC Code]; FluffyWA's practice is two meals for adults
- **Feed puppies under 6 months at least twice daily** [NSW Code No 5] (FluffyWA feeds puppies 3–4 meals by age in months; neonates follow the survival plan's own frequency)
- **Supply fresh, clean water at all times** (uninterrupted); on hot days set out several water bowls in shade to guard against tipping [NSW Code No 5] [RSPCA]
- Prepare food **hygienically, served in clean, tip-resistant containers**; wash food and water bowls daily [NSW Code No 5]
- **Keep to the dog's existing diet; avoid sudden changes of food** (to reduce gastrointestinal upset) [RSPCA]
- **Supervise feeding and drinking and observe every day**, recording each dog's intake and appetite [NSW Code No 5]; record body condition scores periodically [Shelter-medicine best practice]

## 3. Daily Health Check

### Frequency and responsibility

- **Check every dog on site every day** to confirm health and welfare — this is an explicit requirement [NSW Code No 5] [VIC Code] [RSPCA]
- **Check higher-risk dogs more often**: puppies, sick, isolated, post-operative, and elderly dogs [Shelter-medicine best practice]
- If illness/injury is found, **arrange prompt veterinary care**; if an infectious disease is suspected, **isolate immediately** (isolation facilities must be available: on site or at the vet) [NSW Code No 5]

### Daily check-list (run through every dog)

| Observation | What to look for / warning signs |
| --- | --- |
| Appetite / drinking | Finished the meal, refusing food, abnormal drinking |
| Elimination | Frequency, diarrhoea, blood, tenesmus, vomiting |
| Demeanour / behaviour | Dull, hiding, abnormal — often an early sign of illness or injury |
| Movement / gait | Lameness, unwillingness to bear weight, stiffness |
| Coat / skin | Lumps, wounds, fleas and ticks |
| Eyes / nose | Discharge, purulent nasal discharge |
| Respiratory | Coughing, wheezing (kennel cough) |
| Body condition / weight | Too thin / too fat, abnormal change in weight |
| Pain / stress | Pain posture, sustained signs of stress |

> Colour grading follows the **three-colour status method** (green = normal / yellow = observe, recheck in 24h / red = alert, isolate and escalate immediately), consistent with the *Manager Control System*. When in doubt, grade up rather than down.

### Intake and vaccination

- **A vaccination certificate must be presented before intake**, showing vaccination against **distemper, hepatitis, parvovirus, and kennel cough** within the past 12 months (i.e. **C5**) [NSW Code No 5]
- **FluffyWA policy: the C5 must be completed at least 14 days before intake**, per the *Optimal Operations Plan* (immunity takes time to develop; injectable Bordetella protects only 5–7 days after the second dose, intranasal after 48–72 hours). **Frontline staff apply the 14-day rule — a dog short of 14 days is not admitted (offer to rebook).**
- Note that "14 days in advance" is **our own standard, not written into the NSW code**; where a council or vet imposes something stricter, follow the stricter rule [Verify with local council]
- On intake day, perform a **health and behaviour check and record a baseline**; newly arrived dogs should be **observed more closely** (stress lowers immunity and raises susceptibility to infection) [Shelter-medicine best practice] [RSPCA]
- Ensure **timely access to a vet** during boarding, and keep the owner's vet contact details on file [RSPCA]

### Heat stress (a Perth summer priority)

- **Recognise**: sustained rapid panting, fast heartbeat, pale or reddened gums, drooling, vomiting and diarrhoea, weakness and lethargy, muscle tremors, seizures, reduced urination, collapse [RSPCA]
- **Prevent**: provide continuous shade, ventilation, and ample water; avoid hot ground (concrete/sand); schedule exercise only in the coolest periods (early morning / evening), avoiding the hottest part of the day [RSPCA]
- **First aid**: "cool first, then transport" — begin cooling immediately, then take the dog to the vet [RSPCA]

## 4. Putting It Into Practice: A Daily Rhythm Across Four Sites

The framework keeps the two heavy work blocks built around the dogs' routine, plus a duty watch; the four sites execute it to scale (K3 rotates by zone; K4 has a dedicated person for puppies).

| Time block | Cleaning & disinfection | Feeding & water | Health check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 07:00–09:00 morning | Clear faeces/urine as seen, top up water bowls | Check water, prep meals | **First dog-by-dog round of the day** |
| 09:00–12:00 late morning | **While dogs are out: daily clean**; on disinfection days, clean then disinfect | Breakfast; record intake 15 min after feeding | Observe movement/gait/social behaviour during yard time; log yellows and reds |
| 12:00–13:00 midday | Duty watch, clear as seen | Check and top up water | One round, focusing on yellow and red dogs |
| 13:00–16:00 afternoon | Terminal disinfection at turnover; clean the activity yard | Dinner; extra meals for puppies by age | Second round of yard-time observation; on hot days, step up heatstroke checks |
| 16:00–18:00 evening | Final check before closing, bedding, top up water | Evening feed for puppies | Evening dog-by-dog round; hand over the yellow/red list |
| Overnight (K4 on-site) | Clear as seen, keep dry | Overnight puppy feeds by age, water available at all times | Three overnight puppy checks, plus rounds on sick dogs |

> **The order rule (ironclad)**: cleaning and rounds in every time block follow the order "healthy puppies → healthy adults → sick/isolation last"; the isolation zone has a dedicated person and dedicated equipment, with gloves changed on entry and exit.

## Basis and Disclaimer

- **RSPCA**: the Five Domains welfare model, "check for illness and injury daily," heat protection, boarding selection, and veterinary access (kb.rspca.org.au).
- **NSW Code of Practice No 5 (boarding establishments for cats and dogs)**: daily cleaning / weekly disinfection / feeding frequency / daily rounds / intake vaccination — the most specific benchmark in Australia (not mandatory in WA).
- **Victorian boarding code**: daily cleaning and rounds, daily feeding, clean water.
- **Shelter-medicine best practice** (UW-Madison, ASPCApro, etc.): clean before disinfecting, disinfectant selection for parvo, cleaning order and equipment zoning — **not law**.
- ⚠️ Figures are research-compiled; **verify against the official text before writing formal SOPs**; **each of the four sites is governed by its own council's kennel-licence conditions**; clinical thresholds are set by the supervising vet.
